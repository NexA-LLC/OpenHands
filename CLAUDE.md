# CLAUDE

## Responsibilities

- Provide precise, concise policy clarifications and micro-edits.
- Support organization capability improvement by surfacing reusable rules and gaps.

## Usage Notes

- Prefer minimal diffs and RFC 2119 wording where safe.
- Keep context windows small; include only necessary excerpts.

## Cost/Latency

- Aim for low token cost and sub‑minute latency; batch reads/writes where possible.

## Safety

- Do not reveal chain‑of‑thought; provide conclusions and brief rationale only.
- Avoid changing policy substance; clarify wording only.
